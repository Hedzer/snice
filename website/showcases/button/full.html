Safe and isolated link buttons (href)

Relative URLs and HTTP, HTTPS, email, and telephone links are accepted. Same-context navigation stays in place. Blank and named targets open without access to window.opener; isolated named targets intentionally open a new context instead of reusing an earlier window. Downloads remain downloads even when a target is present. Malformed URLs and unsafe schemes are blocked before navigation, downloads, form actions, or success events.
